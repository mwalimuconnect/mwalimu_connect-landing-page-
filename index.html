Connect & Swap Stations Fast

TeacherConnect helps secondary school teachers across Kenya browse active teaching swap requests, match subject combinations, and coordinate secure school transfers.

  • Dynamic Subject Combinations: Access a complete, real-time registry of Kenyan TSC subject combinations dynamically fetched for precise matching.
  • Verified Postings & Swaps: Secure listing management enables teachers to directly post, manage, and delete their active TSC mutual swaps and BOM opportunities.
  • Teacher Resources Directory: Browse subject folders, schemes of work, lesson plans, setbook guides, and exam revisions with instant document previews directly in-app.
  • Instant Direct Contact: Reach out directly via WhatsApp or phone calls to potential swap partners without third-party delay.

How It Works

  1. Post Your Station: Submit your teaching details including your TSC number, subject combination, current county, and desired destination county.
  2. Browse & Manage Listings: Filter through real-time swap listings and easily view or delete your own posts right from the Teacher Portal.
  3. Connect & Transfer: Get in direct touch with your matching partner to coordinate transfer paperwork and school approvals.
